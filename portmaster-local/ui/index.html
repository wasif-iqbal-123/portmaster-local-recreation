    Login autostart

    Install two Windows scheduled tasks: the app starts at login with administrator rights and --system-wide, and a --dns-guard watchdog restores the recorded DNS and browser policy if the app stops, crashes, or is killed.

    Browser secure DNS

    Chromium browsers can bypass OS DNS with their own DNS-over-HTTPS setting. Disabling the enterprise policy for Brave, Chrome, and Edge keeps browser queries on this resolver. The prior policy values are recorded for restoration.
